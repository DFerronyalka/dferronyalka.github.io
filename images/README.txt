Put your project photos in this folder.

Then reference them in index.html like:
    image: "images/my-photo.jpg",

Tips:
- The filename must match EXACTLY, including capital letters.
- Keep each image under ~500 KB. Resize at squoosh.app (free, in-browser).
- Landscape photos (wider than tall) look best - the cards are 16:9.
- No photo yet? Leave image: "" and you get a clean auto-generated tile.
